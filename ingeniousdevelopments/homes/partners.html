Track Record

Starting small. Building proof.

Ingenious Developments launched in Clayton, NC in 2024. Our first work was in furnished short-term rentals — a self-funded operation that generated the operating capital and property management discipline we're now applying to Ingenious Homes.

We're early. We're not going to pretend otherwise. What we have is a clear operational model, an identifiable need in the markets we work in, and a founder with direct ties to these communities who has chosen to build here rather than leave.

What makes us different

We're a small operator with local relationships, a self-sustaining business model in short-term rentals, and a genuine stake in the communities we work in. We're raising capital to accelerate work we've already started.

Market Strategy

Why these markets.

Existing community roots

We target towns where we have real relationships — with contractors, with local officials, with residents. We don't parachute into unfamiliar markets.

Distressed inventory availability

Markets with visible blighted or vacant single-family stock where acquisition cost is reasonable and rehab delivers a genuine improvement to the neighborhood.

Attainable rent achievable

We target markets where a rehabbed property can be rented at a rate reasonable for local workers while still covering operating costs. If the math doesn't work for both sides, we don't pursue it.

Displacement pressure is visible

Communities where longtime residents are at measurable risk of being priced out — not as a theory, but as something you see when you're there.

Growth Plan

1–2 properties per year. Measured growth.

We are not trying to build a hundred-unit portfolio quickly. We're building the operational foundation to manage a small, well-run portfolio and adding properties at a pace where quality doesn't slip.

What we offer partners

Full transparency on every property. Acquisition cost, rehab budget, actual spend, rent set, and any variance — documented and shareable.

Community impact reporting. Who moved in, what they were paying before, what they pay now. We track this because it's the point, not because it's required.

Named contact, not a queue. You will always have a direct line to the founder. Not a pitch deck and a form.

Self-sustaining operating model. The short-term rental side of Ingenious Developments generates operating income. We are not dependent on a single grant to stay alive.

Long-term alignment. We live here. We're not building a portfolio to flip it. This is where we are.

Who we're looking for: CDFIs, community development loan funds, municipal housing programs, faith-based housing organizations, foundations, and individuals with patient capital and mission alignment. If you're optimizing for a 90-day return, we're not the right fit. If you want to be able to point to a neighborhood five years from now and say you helped it stay itself, call us.
