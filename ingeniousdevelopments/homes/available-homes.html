How to Apply

Our process is straightforward.

We screen fairly, communicate directly, and don't charge application fees. Here's how it works.

1

Submit your interest

Fill out the form. Tell us about yourself, your household, and what you're looking for. Existing neighborhood residents are flagged for priority contact.

2

We reach out when a unit matches

We contact you when a property is available in your area that fits what you described. No spam, no automated emails — a real person reaches out.

3

View the property

We show you the home in person. You'll see the full condition, the lease terms, and the rent before committing to anything.

4

Standard screening

We run a standard background and credit check (no application fee). We review the full picture, not just a score — employment history, references, and ability to pay matter too.

We do not require pristine credit. If you have a steady income and a history of paying rent, we want to hear from you. We can explain what we look for on a call.
