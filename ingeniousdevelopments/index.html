Our Story
"He saw firsthand what a neighborhood can do when it holds together and what's lost when it comes apart."

Ingenious Stays was founded by a Louisburg, North Carolina native who grew up in a community that invested in its young people and showed up when families needed support. Years later, while working with distressed properties in Clayton, he saw families being pushed out of the towns they had called home for generations. Ingenious Stays was built to address that challenge.

We approach the work from two directions. First, we provide furnished homes for traveling professionals that feel like homes rather than hotel rooms. Since 2025, we have welcomed more than 40 guests, with traveling professionals among our most frequent. This helps bring skilled workers and local spending into the communities we serve. Second, we reinvest a portion of that income into acquiring and thoughtfully restoring single-family homes and duplexes. We hire local contractors, aim to keep rents reasonable, and help create a real path for longtime residents to remain in the neighborhoods they helped build.

Our work began in Clayton, with plans to expand into Louisburg, Wake Forest, Knightdale, Southeast Raleigh, and Durham. We believe change can be positive, but it should not come at the expense of the people who already call these communities home. Our goal is to build responsibly, uplift our neighbors, and do right by every community we serve.

Mission

Why we exist.

Ingenious Stays provides quality, comfortable housing that strengthens North Carolina communities from both directions welcoming the traveling professionals who bring skills and economic activity into our towns, and rehabilitating homes so longtime residents can stay in the neighborhoods they built. We believe revitalization should lift a community up, not price its people out.
