Our Story

Built with Purpose.
Managed with Care.

Ingenious Stays was founded on a belief that short-term rentals can — and should — feel like a well-considered home. Every property in our portfolio is selected, furnished, and maintained to a standard that makes guests feel respected, not just accommodated.

We serve traveling professionals, medical workers, families in transition, and business travelers who need more than a place to sleep.

How We Started

A Better Stay Was Always the Goal.

Ingenious Stays began with a simple question: why do so many short-term rentals feel like afterthoughts? Mismatched furniture, slow WiFi, unclear check-in instructions — guests were expected to adapt to the property, not the other way around.

We decided to build the kind of stay we'd actually want. Every property in our portfolio is selected, furnished, and managed to a consistent standard — one that makes guests feel like the space was ready for them specifically.

"Our mission is to provide guests with a home-like experience they can count on — and to build a real estate business that creates lasting value for our guests and our community."

Today, Ingenious Stays serves traveling professionals, medical workers, families in transition, and business travelers. We're building a real estate portfolio grounded in hospitality values: attention to detail, responsive communication, and a commitment to quality that shows up in every review.
